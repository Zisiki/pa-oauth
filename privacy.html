Privacy policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026

pa is used only by its owner, with the owner's own Google account. It is not available to other users.

Data accessed

With the owner's consent through Google OAuth, pa reads Gmail messages, creates Gmail drafts, and reads and edits Google Calendar events. It does not send email.

How data is used and stored

Data is fetched on demand to answer the owner's own requests. The OAuth token is stored on the owner's private server with access limited to the owner. pa does not sell, share, or transfer Google user data to third parties, and does not use it for advertising.

pa's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
